## Approval model

The future approval instance is bound to purpose, accountable operator, owner,
application and governance targets, remote/authentication scope, explicit
no-mutation assertion, allowlist/denylist, expiry, target/scope/value-set
fingerprints, and human confirmation. Expiry, stale evidence, target/operator/
scope/value changes, fingerprint mismatch, unresolved approver, credential
leakage, scope escalation, or any ambiguous result fails closed.
